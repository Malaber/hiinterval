Before reporting

A few things worth checking.

No reminder appeared

Open Settings → Scheduled reminders and confirm reminders are enabled, at least one weekday is selected, and iOS notification permission for HiInterval is allowed.

No sound or spoken cue

Check Settings → Cues. Audio cues can be Tones, Spoken, or Silent. Haptics, audio ducking, and the final three-second countdown are configured separately.

Workout timing looks wrong

Include the plan structure, round number, exercise, current phase, and whether you paused, skipped, backgrounded the app, or switched sides immediately before the problem.

Privacy when asking for help

There is no account to look up.

HiInterval stores its workout data locally and does not send analytics or training history to a support backend. If a report needs plan details, include only the information you are comfortable sharing.
