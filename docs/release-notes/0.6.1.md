# App Store notes: 0.6.1

Workout customization now follows your chosen app appearance and uses compact color previews. Try your draft colors and tones in a full training screen with a working timer, pause, skip, restart, and completion. Preview sessions never save to History, and your preferences change only when you tap Save.

Four new sound choices—Chime, Bell, Pulse, and Sweep—join Classic, Bright, and Mellow for each workout cue.
