# App Store notes: 0.3.0 → 0.5.4

## Ready to paste

Build your next workout faster:
• Keep exercises in a shared catalogue, reuse them across plans, and merge duplicates.
• Organize exercises with body areas and tags, with suggestions as you type.
• Generate workouts from your catalogue, filter by tags, and alternate body areas. Exercise order stays consistent across rounds.
• Shuffle a plan’s exercises while keeping its other workout settings.

Make training your own:
• Choose workout icons and colours, or crop a photo into a square logo.
• Customize global workout colours, including distinct round recovery and side-switch phases.
• Enter exact durations by tapping the time.
• Get optional halfway cues that respect your chosen spoken or tones-only mode.

Enjoy a clearer workout screen with stable controls, a floating pause indicator, clearer recovery and side labels, and improved navigation back to warm-up. This update also improves plan scrolling and accessibility and fixes a crash when deleting catalogue exercises.

## Scope

Compared with release-preparation commit `2d93b9d` (0.3.0). Extra rounds and Apple
Intelligence plan editing already existed at that baseline and are intentionally
not presented as new here. Internal CI/test changes are excluded from customer notes.
